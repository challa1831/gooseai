Pre-flight briefing

Questions pilots actually ask.

How fast is 48 hours?

For Lock-On and Dogfight sorties, you'll have a diagnosis + fix + draft appeal in your inbox within 48 hours of forwarding the rejection email. Apple's review queue is the long pole; we cut every other delay.

Can you guarantee my app clears?

For deterministic rejections (Guideline 2.1 performance, 5.1 privacy, banned paywall patterns), yes — Mayday Rescue comes with a money-back guarantee. For subjective rejections (4.3 spam, 3.1.1 business-model), we can't guarantee, because Apple's reviewers themselves don't apply these consistently.

Do you upload my code anywhere?

We never upload binaries. Diagnosis runs on the rejection notice, your store-listing metadata, and screenshots you send us. Code-level fixes are described as patches you apply locally.

Is GooseAI the same as QApilot?

GooseAI is the wedge — per-sortie, urgent, indie-priced. QApilot is the underlying QA platform that prevents the rejection from happening again. Pilots who land with us once usually upgrade into QApilot's Day-0 paywall and Privacy Manifest QA suite.

Do you work with Google Play rejections too?

Affirmative. Radar covers Play policy actions and version-history divergence. Resolution is iOS-first today (where the pain is most acute), with full Play support rolling out in Q3 2026.
