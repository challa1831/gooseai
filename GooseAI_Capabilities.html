Capabilities

Everything you need to clear the queue.

From the first rejection email to the final submission that doesn't bounce. Each capability handles one specific kind of Apple rejection, with a clear input (your rejection notice or store listing) and a clear output (fix, appeal, or pre-submission test plan).

Email-in agent

Forward the rejection notice. Get a guideline-specific diagnosis + fix + appeal-letter draft back in minutes. No login.

Paywall compliance checks

Screenshot scans of your subscription flow against Apple's banned-pattern list. Updated within hours of each policy change.

Privacy Manifest validation

Pre-flight scan for ITMS-91053 auto-rejection risk across your binary's third-party SDKs — before submission, not after.

Appeal-letter drafting

Guideline-specific appeals written in the tone Apple's review board actually responds to. Edit, sign, send.

Day-0 paywall QA (QApilot)

Automated tests across devices, locales, and OS versions for your Day-0 subscription flow — the highest-leverage prevention surface.

Pre-submission test plan

For each rejection we resolve, you get a CI-ready checklist that catches the same class of issue before the next submission.

Hit a wall? Forward the rejection.

Email a rejection notice and we'll match the right capability automatically — Radar for guideline matches, Lock-On for human review, Mayday for paywall and stuck-app rescues.
